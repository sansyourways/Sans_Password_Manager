# SPM Code of Conduct

Version 2.0 — August 2026

## Our commitment

The Sans Password Manager community is committed to a respectful, welcoming,
and security-conscious environment for users, contributors, and maintainers,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and give constructive, technically grounded feedback.
- Welcome newcomers and differing viewpoints.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Protect personal information, vault contents, credentials, keys, and private
  infrastructure details.
- Use synthetic data in reports, tests, screenshots, and demonstrations.
- Follow `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `docs/SECURITY.md`, and the Apache-2.0 license.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, or sexualized conduct.
- Trolling, spam, sustained disruption, or knowingly false security claims.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Posting real credentials, vaults, recovery keys, tokens, or exploit details
  that put users at immediate risk.
- Impersonating maintainers or presenting unofficial builds as official SPM
  releases.

Good-faith security research, source review, forks, modifications, and
redistribution performed in accordance with Apache-2.0 are allowed. Potential
vulnerabilities should be reported privately until a coordinated disclosure is
agreed.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, review channels, events, and public spaces where someone is
officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close interactions, issue
warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement
will consider context, severity, repetition, and community safety.

Report conduct concerns to support@sansyourways.xyz. Report vulnerabilities to
security@sansyourways.xyz. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as
reasonably possible.
