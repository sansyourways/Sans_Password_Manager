## User-controlled data

SPM operates on vaults, passwords, keys, backups, attachments, and other data
selected and controlled by the user. Users are responsible for protecting their
master password, recovery keys, vault files, backups, device, and filesystem
permissions. The maintainers cannot access or recover encrypted vault data.

## No hosted service

The project does not provide a hosted vault, account, telemetry, or recovery
service. Core vault operations are local. Optional update checks contact GitHub
Releases, and users may deliberately configure local features such as web mode
or filesystem synchronization.
